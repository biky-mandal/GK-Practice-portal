⏱️ About the timer

  • The timer is in the top bar. It starts as soon as you tap Start test.
  • The clock runs on our server, so refreshing the page won't reset it.
  • When it reaches zero, your test is submitted automatically. You don't need to do anything.
  • You can leave with Save & exit and come back to continue. The timer pauses while you're away.

✍️ Answering questions

  • Tap an option to choose your answer. It is saved right away.
  • To change it, tap a different option. To remove it, tap Clear my answer.
  • Use Next and Previous to move around, or tap any number in the side panel.
  • Use Mark for review on a question you want to look at again. It doesn't affect your marks.

🎨 Question colours

  • 1 Not opened yet
  • 2 Opened, but not answered
  • 3 Answered
  • 4 Marked for review, not answered
  • 5 Answered and marked for review: your answer is counted

"Mark for review" only helps you find a question again. A marked question with no answer earns no marks.

✅ Finishing

  • Tap Finish test on the last question to submit and see your result.
  • Unanswered questions are simply counted as not attempted.
  • English and Assamese are shown together wherever available.

Please read carefully

  • Each question has several answer options. Choose the one you think is best.
  • All questions carry equal marks.
  • You may leave a question unanswered. Only answered questions are scored.
