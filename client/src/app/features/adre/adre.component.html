About ADRE 3.0

The Assam Direct Recruitment Examination (ADRE) is the Government of Assam’s large-scale recruitment process for Grade-III and Grade-IV vacancies across state departments. ADRE 2.0 was conducted in 2024 for 12,673 vacancies, and appointment letters were distributed on 9 and 10 January 2026.

The ADRE 3.0 notification is expected after the Assam Assembly elections (April to May 2026), as confirmed by the Chief Minister. Reports suggest 10,000 to 15,000 vacancies for both Grade-III and Grade-IV.

Syllabus status: the official ADRE 3.0 syllabus has not been released. Details below follow ADRE 2.0 (syllabus released 15 July 2024) and the SLRC framework. ADRE 3.0 may introduce changes, including a two-phase (Preliminary and Main) exam. This page will be updated when SLRC publishes the official syllabus.
