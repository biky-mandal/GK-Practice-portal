Before you begin

Check eligibility by post

The supplied notice requires Indian citizenship, Assam residency, local-language proficiency and Assam Employment Exchange registration. Education and age limits differ, including post-specific relaxations. Use the official notification for your post.

Written preparation is one stage

Constable selection also includes document checks, physical efficiency and standards, medical checks and an oral stage. These Test Series cover written practice only, not physical training or a selection guarantee.

Language and availability

The supplied notice lists Assamese, Bodo, Bengali and English for the official Constable paper. This platform supports English and Assamese content. Mock papers become playable after all 100 active questions are published.
