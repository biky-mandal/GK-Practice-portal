What deletion does

After you verify the code sent to your registered email, we delete your account and its application records in one database transaction. This includes your profile, practice attempts and answers, scores, test history, pack purchases and payment records held by this application, reviews, question reports, refresh sessions and contact messages linked to the account email.

If you cannot access your registered email or need help with a deletion request, contact support@letscrackadre.com.

  • Deletion is performed after email verification; the request form alone does not delete anything.
  • A pseudonymous audit record is configured to expire after 90 days; MongoDB TTL cleanup is asynchronous and may occur later.
  • Razorpay, email recipients, infrastructure logs and backups may hold separate copies under their own or deployment retention settings. The application does not set a fixed expiry period for those copies.
